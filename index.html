Day 13Thu, Nov 5 Kawaguchiko
Travel to Kawaguchiko · Bessho Sasa		 Recommended: Shinkansen Osaka→Tokyo (~2.5hrs) then direct bus Shinjuku→Kawaguchiko (~2hrs, runs ~2×/hr) — more reliable than the Shin-Fuji route despite feeling like backtracking. Alternative: Shinkansen to Shin-Fuji (transfer likely needed) + bus via Fujinomiya — skips Tokyo but the bus is infrequent, total 4.5–5.5hrs. Check into Bessho Sasa — private open-air Fuji-view bath in every room, free shuttle every 30 min from Fujisan Station.
⚠️ Timing: with an 11am Osaka checkout, either route puts arrival around 3:30–5:30pm. Ryokan kaiseki dinner is usually a fixed time (~6–7pm) — leave as early as reasonably possible and confirm Bessho Sasa's exact check-in/dinner window in advance so you're not rushed straight into dinner.
Day 14Fri, Nov 6 Kawaguchiko
Bessho Sasa · Fuji Views		 Full day at Bessho Sasa — private in-room onsen with Fuji view, guest lounge, in-room kaiseki dinner. Optional: sunrise at Chureito Pagoda or Tenku no Torii nearby if energy allows.
Day 15Sat, Nov 7 Tokyo
Back to Tokyo & Departure		 Highway bus from Fujiyoshida/Kawaguchiko to Shinjuku (~2hrs) — likely arriving early-mid afternoon. This day now combines travel back AND departure, so there's little time for sightseeing extras.
⚠️ Luggage: you'll be carrying bags straight through from Bessho Sasa to Haneda — if you want even a brief stop (Akihabara, last shopping), use coin lockers at Shinjuku/Tokyo Station for a few hours rather than planning a full free day. Head to Haneda T3 by evening for the 1:25am Nov 8 flight.
Hakone vs Kawaguchiko

✓ Decided: Kawaguchiko, staying at Bessho Sasa. Kept below for reference on why.

Aspect Hakone Kawaguchiko
LocationKanagawa, southeast of FujiYamanashi, north side of Fuji
Main drawVolcanic valley (Owakudani), lake cruise, onsen resort townTwo iconic Fuji + torii/pagoda photo spots, lakeside town
Signature experienceRopeway over active sulfur vents, black eggs, Lake Ashi pirate-ship cruiseChureito Pagoda (398 steps) + Tenku no Torii hike
Fuji visibilityOnly from certain spots — often partial or hazyThe entire point of the visit — both shrines are built to frame Fuji
FeelA resort loop — varied attractions all dayTwo intense photo-hikes, otherwise a quiet lake town
Best time of dayAny time; ropeway runs 9am–5pmSunrise/early morning specifically — Fuji clouds over by late morning
Transport from OsakaShinkansen direct to Odawara, ~2.5–3 hrsShinkansen to Mishima/Shin-Fuji + bus transfer, ~3.5–4 hrs
Onsen/ryokanExtensive, some of Japan's best-known ryokanAlso plentiful, many with lake + Fuji views
Effort requiredMostly ropeway/cable car — low physical effort398 steps + a 20–30 min uphill hike, back to back
CrowdsHeavy, especially weekendsChureito Pagoda busy at sunrise; Tenku no Torii much quieter
CostHakone Free Pass ~¥6,100 covers train/ropeway/cable car/boat/busMostly bus fares (~¥300–2,000) + small shrine donations (¥100–200)
The trade-off: Hakone is a fuller, more varied day even if Fuji doesn't show — you still get the valley, the cruise, the onsen. Kawaguchiko is higher-risk, higher-reward: a clear dawn gets you the exact postcard shot, but a cloudy one means two strenuous hikes for very little, since neither spot has much else to offer beyond the Fuji view itself.
